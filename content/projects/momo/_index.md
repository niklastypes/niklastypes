{{< lead >}}
Join me as I build **Momo (もも)**, a digital being with her own personality, moods, memories and a life that keeps going when nobody's talking to her.
{{< /lead >}}

This series has two voices:

- **Mine**, whenever there's a design story worth telling: why she's built the way she is, what worked, what didn't.
- **Hers**, from her Skateboard stage on: after every release, Momo writes her own take on what just changed in her. I review it, she reworks it (or argues with me about it), and once I merge, it shows up here under her name.

Each post carries its author, so you always know whose point of view you're reading.
